What WeighCast is

WeighCast reads your weigh-ins from Apple Health, works out the trend underneath the day-to-day variation, and estimates a range of dates by which you might reach a weight you have chosen.

What WeighCast is not

WeighCast is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and it does not provide medical, nutritional or clinical advice. It cannot distinguish muscle from fat from water — it knows only your total weight.

Its estimates are projections from past readings, not predictions of what will happen. They will change as you add weigh-ins, and they may be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical decisions. Speak to a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to how you eat or exercise, and particularly if you have a health condition.

Healthy weight limits

WeighCast will not accept a target weight below a healthy range for your height. This limit is deliberate and cannot be turned off.

If you are struggling with your weight, eating or body image, please consider speaking to someone. In the UK, Beat offers support at beateatingdisorders.org.uk. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders can be found at allianceforeatingdisorders.com.
