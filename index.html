WeighCast

Weight loss without knowing when it ends is hard. WeighCast reads your weigh-ins from Apple Health, finds the trend underneath the daily noise, and estimates when you'll reach your goal.

  • iPhone
  • Free
  • No adverts, ever
  • All on your device

What it does

  • Follows your trend weight rather than any single reading, because day-to-day swings of a kilo or two are mostly water and food.
  • Gives you a range of dates rather than a single day, and shows you honestly how certain it is.
  • Marks out milestones along the way, so there's a win every few weeks instead of one distant payoff.
  • Works with a smart scale that syncs to Apple Health, or with weigh-ins you add by hand.

What it won't do

  • No adverts, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing sold to anyone.
  • No streaks or nagging — a missed day isn't a failure.
  • No targets below a healthy weight range for your height.
  • It can't tell muscle from fat, and it won't pretend otherwise.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers, so there's nowhere for your information to go. The only things WeighCast writes to Apple Health are weigh-ins you type in yourself.
