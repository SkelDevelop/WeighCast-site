# WeighCast

Weight loss without knowing when it ends is hard. **WeighCast** reads your
weigh-ins from Apple Health, finds the trend underneath the day-to-day noise,
and estimates when you'll reach your goal.

An iPhone app. Free, no adverts, everything stays on your device.

## What the app does

- Follows your **trend weight** rather than any single reading, because daily
  swings of a kilo or two are mostly water and food.
- Gives a **range of dates** rather than a single day, and shows honestly how
  certain it is.
- Marks **milestones along the way**, so there's a win every few weeks instead
  of one distant payoff.
- Works with a smart scale that syncs to Apple Health, or with weigh-ins you add
  by hand.

## What it deliberately doesn't do

- No adverts, no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold to anyone.
- No streaks or nagging — a missed day isn't a failure.
- No targets below a healthy weight range for your height.
- No claims about muscle or body fat. It knows your total weight and says so.

## Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers, so
there's nowhere for your information to go. The only things WeighCast writes
back to Apple Health are weigh-ins and a height you typed in yourself.
